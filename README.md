CampusOS

«A digital operating system for university life.»

CampusOS is a multi-school platform that brings academics, opportunities, student communities, and campus information into one organized experience.

Instead of students relying on scattered portals, WhatsApp groups, emails, notice boards, and social media, CampusOS brings the relevant information together in one place.

V1

CampusOS V1 focuses on the core student experience:

Study + Career + Social + Campus

The platform is designed to support multiple universities from the beginning. University-specific information is shown based on the student's institution, while relevant public opportunities can remain available across schools.

CampusOS complements official university systems rather than replacing them. For example, official course registration remains on the university portal while CampusOS helps students understand and organize their academic requirements.
